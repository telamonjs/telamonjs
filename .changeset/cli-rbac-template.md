New apps ship database-backed RBAC. The template's Prisma schema replaces the `RoleName` enum with the RBAC tables of `@telamonjs/permissions` (`Role`, `Permission`, `RolePermission`, `UserRole`); `User` gets `roles`, `active` (was `ativo`), `accessChangedAt` and `sessionsRevokedAt`. The seed syncs a code-defined permission catalog (`src/rbac.ts`) with `diffPermissionCatalog`, and creates a built-in `admin` role holding every permission plus the admin user with it. Sign-in materializes roles and permission codes into the session token; on every renewal `sessionState` signs out deactivated or revoked users and refreshes stale claims. The single app gets `/admin/papeis` (role CRUD and a `PermissionMatrix` tab), multi-role assignment and activate/deactivate in `/admin/usuarios`, a `PermissionsProvider` around the shell, menu and CrudConfig permissions, and `requirePermission()` in every Route Handler. The monorepo moves `src/rbac.ts` to `packages/shared` (`./rbac`) and replaces the API's `AdminGuard`/`@AdminOnly()` with a global `createPermissionsGuard` and `@Permissions(...)`, plus a `roles` module (CRUD, `GET roles/matrix`, grant/revoke). Existing apps are unaffected.
